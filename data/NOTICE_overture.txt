NOTICE — Foursquare OS Places records in this repository (Apache License, Version 2.0)

Where: `data/overture_places.json` (the 151 records of `places[]` whose `sources[0].dataset` is "Foursquare") and
`data/poi.enriched.json` (45 records whose `overture_id` points at one of them: 28 Overture-only records and 17 OSM
records that took a phone / website / brand from it; the fields are marked `field_sources.<field>.contributor =
"Foursquare"`, `license = "Apache-2.0"`). Count date 2026-10-17, Overture release 2026-09-23.1.

How the data reached us: not from Foursquare directly but through Overture Maps (places theme), which states
(https://docs.overturemaps.org/attribution/, read 2026-10-17):
  "Data from Foursquare. Copyright 2024 Foursquare Labs, Inc. All rights reserved. Available under Apache 2.0.
   Foursquare data was transformed to the Overture schema. Changed: 2026-03-18. NOTICE.txt."
The "NOTICE.txt" link of that page points to the page reproduced below.

Notice of changes (Apache-2.0 section 4(b)): the Foursquare data was transformed to the Overture schema by Overture
(see above); we then (collect/overture_places.py, collect/merge.py) cut it to the Astana bounding box, kept the
fields we use, mapped the categories to our own, applied a confidence cut-off of 0.7, merged matching records with
OpenStreetMap places and attached them to our own record structure. The files carrying these records are changed
files and are not Foursquare's original data.
