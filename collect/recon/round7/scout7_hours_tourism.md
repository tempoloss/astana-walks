## Wins

**Does any paid tier legally let us store hours for Astana venues?** Yes on paper, no in practice, and the two failures are different:

1. **OpenTripMap is the only vendor whose retention clause is a real, readable "you may store it"** — «You can pre-fetch, index, store, or cache data», ODbL, no 24h/30-day clock, no derivative-works ban, no territory limit, and even the free tier is quota-clear at 5000 req/day (commercial just needs the paid plan, ≈$19/mo, i.e. ~270× cheaper than the $5400/mo Google escape). It does **not** flip our verdict because **its schema has no opening_hours field at all** (checked every property of `components.schemas.Place` in the published OpenAPI). It would be a descriptions/wikidata-bridge source, and we already pull both from OSM+Wikidata+Wikipedia directly, license-clean.
2. **Foursquare Premium does have exactly our field** (`hours`: JSON day→[open,close] in local time, plus `rating`, `price`, `photos`) but it is *not a purchasable self-service tier*: «Early Access Only / Fill out the Contact Us form», and the API-side terms prohibit building a POI dataset from the output. ⇒ **CONTRACT**, not STORE-paid.
3. **The one genuine new legal storable hours feed: `e-museum.kz` WP REST** — $0, keyless, one request per region, structured `work_hours` + `ticket_price` + 3-language names for the Ministry-of-Culture museum register. Effect on the shipped dataset: **+4 Astana museums** (Нацмузей, Сейфуллин, ВИМ ВС РК, «Бозоқ») — and, per our own `hours.analyze` rule, those strings name no weekdays, so they land in `hours_unqualified`, **not** in coverage %. Real payoff is `ticket_price` + KK/EN names for museums that have no own domain (exactly the 22 `manual_targets` shape).

So: **the hours hole has no mythical API and this round killed the last three candidates for it.** Concrete engineering path, sized on the shipped `data/poi.enriched.json` (2316 records):

| …of which in the 4 most-recommended categories | **54** (landmark 12, museum 9, theatre 18, entertainment 15) | priority queue — the weighted hole |
| …of which `attraction_landmark`+`museum_gallery` only | 21 of 152 (96.7 % of that pair has no hours) | |

## Dead/rejected

- **OpenTripMap** — as a *hours* source: DEAD, no field (not a licensing problem). As a commercial data source it would additionally put a Moscow-registered LLC (ООО «АПМЭПС», ИНН 7751169420, Russian-law offer, Moscow arbitration venue) inside a Kazakh commercial product's data lineage, and its own terms push ODbL share-alike onto us — the same obligation our OSM layer already carries. Marketing domain `opentripmap.io` is parked for sale.
- **openingh.openstreetmap.de evaluation tool** — client-side JS, no REST endpoint (raw HTML verified); **api.openinghours.com**, **openinghours.to** — `ENOTFOUND`; **osm2world / overpass-turbo opening_hours presets** — editors/query templates, no service. There is no hosted OSM-hours resolver to buy: the library is ours (`opening-hours-py`, `collect/sites/hours.py`), the *data* is the OSM tag we already hold.
- **Foursquare OS Places `hours`/`timezone`** — re-verified against the current 26-column schema: still no hours column. Round 4 stands.
- **visitastana.kz** — `/sitemap.xml` and `/sitemap_index.xml` both HTTP 500; round 2's SEO-spam JSON-LD finding unchanged.
- **Yandex/2GIS** — 30-day cache ceiling verbatim (§5.1.5) ⇒ runtime; 2GIS `/terms` 403s automated fetches, Yandex read in full and generalizes.
- **CommonCrawl as a JSON-LD shortcut** — the index answers URL captures only; there is no hosted `OpeningHoursSpecification` extraction service for .kz. The crawl is the work.
