## Wins

(a) **Is there a real-monitor PM2.5 source for Astana at all? — YES, proven.** The **US Embassy Astana PM2.5 reference monitor** exists and its hourly data is downloadable **keyless, right now**, as CSV from `https://s3-us-west-1.amazonaws.com/files.airnowtech.org/airnow/EmbassyHistorical/Astana/2024/Astana_PM2.5_2024_01_MTD.csv` (2018, 2019, 2022, 2023, 2024 full years; ends **2025-03-04** — feed went dark after March 2025; today's AirNow `HourlyAQObs` files contain no Astana row). This is the authoritative answer OpenAQ's `kz.json` confirms (`StateAir_Astana, active: true`). **Current** real-monitor data: only via aqicn.org's live mirror of 5–6 Kazhydromet automatic posts + embassy page (RUNTIME, no licence to store), or KazHydroMet PDF bulletins (scrapeable facts, Приказ №267), or meteo.kazhydromet.kz ДЭМ behind registration (bulk = paid contract).

(b) **Best legal substitute and cost.** Two-tier, both free: (1) **Historical depth**: ingest the AirNow EmbassyHistorical Astana CSVs (cost: $0; confirm AirNow-Tech terms — US-gov data). For ongoing KazHydroMet coverage, the practical legal path is **AirData.kz** (the only existing cleaned KazHydroMet harvest, 1.7M Astana hourly readings) — but it is **CC BY-NC**, so for a commercial product you must email airdatakz@gmail.com for a license; alternatively enter the KazHydroMet paid contract («на договорной основе», pricelist). (2) **Live overlay**: our own computed smog index (below) at $0, optionally aqicn RUNTIME widget (free token) for a live embassy/KGMT number with deep link.

(c) **Can METAR FU/visibility + satellite AOD be the honest product signal? — YES, and it is the most legally honest option.** All three inputs are public-domain/CC and keyless: METAR UACC visibility + present-weather `FU` (NOAA IEM, already integrated, US-gov public domain) + **GIBS `MODIS_Terra_Aerosol` daily AOD tile** with the exact keyless URL `https://gibs.earthdata.nasa.gov/wmts/epsg3857/best/MODIS_Terra_Aerosol/default/{date}/GoogleMapsCompatible_Level6/{z}/{y}/{x}.png` (Astana = tile 5/22/10; fetched live, HTTP 200 PNG) + optional `TROPOMI_L2_Nitrogen_Dioxide_Tropospheric_Column` same template (also fetched live) or full NetCDF via Planetary Computer STAC `sentinel-5p-l2-netcdf` / CDSE STAC with a free key. A «smog-index» we compute ourselves from these (visibility drop + AOD anomaly + NMU bulletin from Kazhydromet, already known) is attribution-clean, storable, and commercially exploitable — strictly more honest than republishing a third-party AQ API. Caveat to state in-product: AOD/NO2 are column proxies, not ground PM2.5; present it as a smog/visibility index, cite «Contains modified Copernicus Sentinel data» for S5P and NASA GIBS for AOD.
