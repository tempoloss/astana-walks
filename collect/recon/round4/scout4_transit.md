### Cheapest legal way to ship the two acceptance features
**"Bus/LRT stop + last departure":** OSM Overpass `highway=bus_stop` (948) + the two `route=light_rail` LRT relations for line/station geometry (ODbL, STORE) → match to the CC BY 4.0 Zenodo `stops.txt` (173) for canonical stop names and route coverage. For last departure: LRT = fixed rule 06:00–23:00 with "last trains terminate at Әуежай / Нұрлы жол at 23:00; per-station last departure differs — verify at station" (source: CTS via Kazinform, inform.kz cf8913fd, plus the airport's own bus page "Автобустардың жұмыс уақыты 6:00 бастап – 23:00 дейін", №10 last 22:24, №12 last 22:30 — store those three strings). Do **not** promise minute-level bus times: no legal Astana GTFS-RT exists; only a CTS/Автобыс contract unlocks it (endpoint already proven at HTTP 401). UI wording: "остановка nearby (OSM, ODbL) · LRT работает до 23:00 · автобус №12 с аэропорта — до 22:30 · точное время последней электрички уточняйте на станции".
**"Is this street lit":** one Overpass query per walk (or one `kazakhstan-latest.osm.pbf` extract already in the pipeline): flag segment `lit=yes` → "освещено"; `lit=no` → "не освещено" (rare); untagged → no claim, and instead fall back to Open-Meteo sunset time (already integrated) + the Astana-REC weekly outage bulletin (astrec.kz) to warn "возможно отключение освещения на <street> <date> <hours>". Total cost: ODbL attribution + one scraper, no contracts, no keys.
