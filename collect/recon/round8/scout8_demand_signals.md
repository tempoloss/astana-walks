## Wins
1. **Wikipedia pageviews = the measured demand curve (day 1).** Join via Wikidata sitelinks (`wbgetclaims P856`/SPARQL) → for each POI with a ru-wiki article get monthly views. Confirmed numbers: Байтерек 6 510, Хан Шатыр 3 286, Астана Опера 586 (мес). Hit-rate on the 20-name sample = 50 %. Even that 50 % is enough to swap the «simulated» label for a real one on the city slide: «индекс внимания» (pageviews нормированные по территории) + OSM edit velocity (ohsome +18 %/2 г). Both CC0, keyless, storable. Replaces: simulated 10 000 synthetic requests.
2. **Autocomplete = eval corpus + kk fix + demand phrasings (day 1).** Google `suggestqueries.../complete/search?client=firefox&hl={ru|kk}&gl=kz&q=...` + Yandex `suggest-ff.cgi` (both HTTP 200 keyless, no explicit caching ban). Recipe: **10 seed verbs × 2 languages × 5 tenses** («куда сходить/что посмотреть/где погулять/тихое место/с ребенком» RU; «қайда баруға/көрікті жерлер/мuseum/сәби» KK) → ~100 requests (1 rps, ~2 min) → ~1 000 phrasings → dedup → 40 real eval cases (of which 15–20 kk — fixes the 2-case gap). Also gives real popularity signal by which completions Google/Yandex surface at all.
3. **Velobike 2000+ active users** — citable, verbatim, real-world measured flow for the city slide.
